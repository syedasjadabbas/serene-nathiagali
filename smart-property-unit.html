What is a Smart Property Unit?

A revolutionary way to invest in premium real estate. With just 50 sq ft per unit, you co-own a luxury apartment in one of Nathia Gali’s most sought-after developments — and on completion, DM Consortium rents it out and distributes the rental income according to your units.
